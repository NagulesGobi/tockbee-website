Privacy Policy

Tockbee is made by dotXline LTD. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Data not collected

Tockbee has no account, no server, no analytics and no third-party tools that collect data. Your shifts, pay, work sites and settings stay in the app’s own storage on your phone.

Location

If you choose clock-in by location, Tockbee uses your phone’s region monitoring around the work sites you create, to clock you in and out. There is no continuous tracking, and your location is never sent off your phone. You can use your work Wi-Fi or the Clock button instead.

Backups and exports

Backups go only to a folder you pick, such as your own iCloud Drive. Timesheets you export (PDF or CSV) go wherever you choose to share or save them.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. If you email us, we keep your message to reply.
